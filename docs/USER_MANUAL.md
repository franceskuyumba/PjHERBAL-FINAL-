# PJHERBAL Clinic – User Manual (Customers)

## 1. Getting started

Open the website. You can browse and buy **without an account** (guest checkout),
but creating a free account unlocks:

- Order history and invoice downloads
- Wishlist
- Saved delivery addresses
- Reviews & ratings
- Personalized product recommendations

### Create an account
1. Click **Sign in** in the top bar → **Create account**.
2. Enter your name, email, and password, then submit.
3. You are signed in immediately (no email verification required in this version).

## 2. Shopping

1. Use **Search** (magnifier icon) or browse **Shop → Categories**.
2. On a product page:
   - Choose quantity and click **Add to cart**.
   - Click **Buy now on WhatsApp** to order directly via WhatsApp.
   - See tabs for Description, How to use, Ingredients, Benefits, and Reviews.
3. Cart (bag icon): adjust quantities, apply a coupon code, and see delivery estimates.

## 3. Checkout

1. **Your details** – name, email, phone (WhatsApp number, e.g. `2557XXXXXXXX`).
2. **Delivery** – select your **Region** (delivery fee updates automatically),
   then district and street address.
   - Add more items to reach **TZS 200,000** and delivery is **FREE**.
3. **Payment** – choose M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, or bank transfer.
4. Confirm. If paying manually, you'll see payment instructions and a
   **Confirm payment on WhatsApp** button.

## 4. After ordering

- **Order number**: noted on the confirmation screen and in your dashboard.
- **Invoice/Receipt**: open the order from **My Orders** → **Invoice / Receipt (PDF)**;
  use your browser's print dialog to save as PDF.
- **Payment confirmation**: complete the transfer, then confirm via WhatsApp.
  Staff mark the order **Paid** and update it through **Delivered**.

## 5. My Dashboard

| Section | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Overview** | Order statuses, wishlist count, recommendations |
| **My Orders** | Order list, status timeline, invoice links |
| **Wishlist** | Products you saved; add to cart from here |
| **Addresses** | Save/manage delivery addresses (set a default) |
| **Reviews** | Reviews you've written |
| **Notifications** | Order updates, promotions, low-stock alerts |
| **Settings** | Update name/phone; change password |

## 6. Getting help

- **Chatbot** (bottom-right): quick answers about products, delivery, and payments.
- **Live support**: tap the support button for a WhatsApp chat with a specialist.
- **Contact page**: message form, phone, email, and Google Maps location.

## 7. Tips

- Orders over **TZS 200,000** get free delivery — watch the progress bar at checkout.
- Subscribe to the newsletter for offers.
- If you leave items in your cart for 2+ hours, a gentle reminder appears so you don't forget.
